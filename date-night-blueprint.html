DATE #1 📍 Go-Out

Park Picnic

Easy, classic, slow.

Pack food, a blanket, and a few hours. Go to the nearest park. Eat on the ground. Stay long enough that you start lying back instead of sitting up.

Make It Happen

Pack sandwiches, fruit, snacks, drinks, dessert. Real plates if you can. Blanket for sitting. Stay at least 90 minutes.

Pro Tips

Skip the public picnic tables, find a quiet patch of grass. Bring a book to read aloud from or a card deck. Phones away.

Perfect For

Spring and summer weekends, anniversaries, when you want a date that costs nothing and feels intentional.

DATE #2 🏠 Stay-In

Wood-Fired Pizza Night at Home

Hands-on, creative, satisfying.

Buy a pizza stone and a dough kit or premade dough. Build pizzas together with your favorite toppings. Bake them blazing hot in the oven or grill. Eat fresh, take photos, compete on prettiest pie.

Make It Happen

Pizza stone preheated 30 minutes at max oven temperature. Pre-stretched dough, sauce, three cheeses, six toppings. Each builds two pizzas. Bake one at a time. Eat between rounds.

Pro Tips

A pizza peel makes the transfer easier. Less is more on toppings. Watch one pizza all the way through to gauge time before the next.

Perfect For

Weekend nights, when you want a hands-on date that ends with a real meal.

Time: 2.5 hours • Budget: $20-50 • Location: At Home

DATE #4 🏠 Stay-In

Movie From Your Childhood

Nostalgic, playful, warm.

Each of you picks one movie from your childhood the other has never seen. Watch back to back. Childhood snacks. The whole point is letting each other see what shaped you when you were small.

Make It Happen

Pick the two movies a few days ahead so you can pause and find them. Get snacks you each ate as a kid. Watch in order. Pause to explain context if you need to.

Pro Tips

Watch yours first since you'll be more eager to share. Tell stories about watching the movie growing up. The movie matters less than the stories around it.

Perfect For

Weekend nights, anniversaries of meeting, when you want to feel like you again.

DATE #7 🏠 Stay-In

Handwritten Letter Exchange

Slow, vulnerable, romantic.

Each of you writes the other a letter, then reads them aloud over coffee, tea, or dessert. Not a quick note. A real letter. Something you'd want them to keep.

Make It Happen

Set 45 minutes aside in separate rooms. Write at least one full page each. Topic prompts if you need them. What you noticed about them this year. The moment you knew. What you want for them in the next chapter. Come back together, swap, read aloud.

Pro Tips

Use real paper and a pen, not a screen. Slow handwriting forces real thought. Don't edit yourself, write the messy version.

Perfect For

Anniversaries, hard seasons that need a reset, or anytime you've been physically together but emotionally drifted.

Time: 2 hours • Budget: $20-50 • Location: At Home

DATE #9 🏠 Stay-In

Cheese Board Tasting

Sophisticated, sensory, easy.

Three cheeses, three pairings. Hard cheese, soft, and blue. Pair with wine, sparkling cider, kombucha, or fancy sodas. Take notes on what worked and what didn't. The point is paying attention together.

Make It Happen

Buy cheeses across hard, soft, and blue. Pick three pairing drinks based on what you actually like. Add crackers, fruit, nuts, honey. Try each cheese with each drink. Pick favorites.

Pro Tips

Let cheeses sit at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving. Ask the cheese counter person for help and tell them the budget. Mocktail pairings work as well as wine.

Perfect For

Weekend nights, when you want to feel grown up without leaving the house.

Time: 2.5 hours • Budget: $50-100 • Location: At Home
